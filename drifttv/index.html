DriftTV

A native player for your own Jellyfin server on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. In development.

  • Continue Watching, Next Up and your whole library in one place
  • Choose quality, audio, subtitles and playback speed while you watch
  • Downloads on iPhone and iPad, when your server allows them
  • Several accounts and servers, with a profile picker
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking

You need the address of a Jellyfin server and an account on it. DriftTV does not include or sell any movies, shows or music. Requires iOS, iPadOS or tvOS 26 or later.

Support · Privacy policy

DriftTV is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Jellyfin project.
