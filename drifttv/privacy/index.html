DriftTV privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

DriftTV is made by Rose Home Software. The app does not send your personal data to us, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Your Jellyfin server

DriftTV connects to the Jellyfin server whose address you enter. To sign you in and play media, it sends that server your sign-in details, your device's name, a random identifier for the app on that device, and what you search for, play, download and mark as watched or favorite. The server is run by you or by someone who gave you an account, and what it keeps is up to its owner. If you use a plain http:// address, this traffic is not encrypted.

Seerr and artwork

If you choose to connect a Jellyseerr or Overseerr server, your searches and requests also go to that server, using the API key you enter. Posters for those titles load from image.tmdb.org (The Movie Database), which receives your IP address and the images requested.

What stays on your device

Sign-in tokens, and a Seerr API key if you add one, are stored in the device's Keychain. Settings, saved server addresses and user names, cached artwork and any downloads are stored on the device. On Apple TV, the titles shown in the Top Shelf are stored there too. Your password is not stored.

Deleting your data

Signing out, or removing an account, deletes that account's token from the device. Remove a Seerr connection to delete its key. Deleting the app deletes its settings, artwork and downloads; sign out first, because the system may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted. For data on a server, ask that server's owner.

TestFlight and the App Store

Apple may give us crash reports, usage information and any feedback you send through TestFlight or the App Store. We use it only to fix and improve the app. Apple's own privacy policy covers what Apple collects.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply and to fix the problem. To have your messages deleted, or to ask anything about this policy, write to rosehomesoftware@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on our website and in the app, with a new date.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security.
