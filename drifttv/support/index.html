DriftTV support

Email rosehomesoftware@gmail.com and tell us your device, its software version, and what happened.

I can’t connect to my server

Enter the same address you use in a web browser, including https:// if your server uses it. At home, allow DriftTV to use your local network in Settings. Away from home, your server needs an address you can reach from the internet or a VPN.

I don’t have a server or an account

DriftTV plays media from a Jellyfin server that you or someone you know runs. Ask its owner for the address and an account. You can learn about running your own at jellyfin.org.

I don’t see a download button

Downloads are for iPhone and iPad, and the server’s owner can turn them off for an account. After a change on the server, quit and reopen DriftTV.

How do I remove my data?

Sign out in DriftTV, then delete the app. See the privacy policy.
